Name lockups, eight marks, a few extras

"Inbox Sift" is a good fit: GitHub already calls the page your Inbox, and "sift" is the action. The prototype and handoff now say Inbox Sift. The only real question is whether "GitHub" belongs in the name itself. GitHub's trademark guidelines generally discourage putting its name at the front of a product name, while "for GitHub" as a descriptor is common and usually fine.
